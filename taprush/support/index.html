TapRush: Beat the Clock — Support

Tips and answers based on how TapRush actually works. For privacy disclosures, see the TapRush Privacy Policy. Questions: pompiko.support@gmail.com.

Getting Started

TapRush is a portrait arcade reflex game. Clear each level by tapping the correct targets as quickly as you can. Your clear time earns up to 3 stars.

  • Campaign introduces mechanics stage by stage (large easy targets first; later stages add lives, precision, hazards, and more).
  • Infinity Rush unlocks after you clear Campaign level 200 (or otherwise reach Infinity levels) and continues through higher level numbers (up to 1,000 playable levels).
  • Soft tutorials appear the first time a new mechanic shows up — read them, then tap Got It to play.
  • From Home, use Continue to jump back into your next unlocked level, or open Select Stage / Level to pick a level.
  • Progress (stars, unlocks, best times, settings) is saved on your device. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I play TapRush?

Tap the glowing targets in the play area. Avoid hazards when they appear (for example bombs or decoys in later levels). The timer starts when you land your first successful hit. Finish all required targets to clear the level, then check your time and star rating on the results screen. You can Retry any level for free.

2. How do I unlock new levels?

Clearing a level unlocks the next one in sequence. Stars and best times are recorded per level so you can improve later. Use Stage / Level select to revisit cleared levels or jump to your next unlocked challenge. Infinity content unlocks after the Campaign requirements are met in-game.

3. How does the lives system work?

Early Campaign levels (Stage 1) do not use lives. From Stage 2 onward, levels typically start with 3 hearts. Misses and certain hazard hits cost a life. When you run out of lives, the run pauses and you can choose a continue option (if available in your build), Retry the level for free, or leave the run.

4. How can I earn extra lives?

On Out of Lives, when continues are enabled for your platform build, you may be offered:

  • A rewarded advertisement — watch to completion to receive continue lives for that attempt.
  • Optional in-app life packs (consumable continues) processed by the App Store or Google Play — TapRush never asks for your card number inside the game.

Retry remains free. Paid continues grant temporary extra lives within the current run; they are not a permanent unlock of all future lives.

5. What happens if a rewarded advertisement is unavailable?

If an ad cannot start, needs privacy consent, has no fill, or fails to finish, TapRush does not grant lives. You’ll see an in-game message such as checking your connection, reviewing Ad Privacy in Settings, trying again later, or skipping to a paid continue when purchases are available. You can always Retry or exit without paying.

6. What should I do if my purchase doesn't appear?

Purchases are confirmed by Apple or Google, then verified by TapRush before lives are granted. If payment is still processing, wait for the store confirmation. If nothing appears after a successful charge:

  • Keep the app open on the same device and wait a moment for recovery.
  • Check that you are signed into the same App Store / Play account used to buy.
  • Force-quit and reopen TapRush once on the same device.
  • If lives still do not appear, contact support with your device details, approximate purchase time, product (life pack size), and platform — do not send passwords, full card numbers, or raw purchase receipts containing secrets.
7. How do I report a bug?

Use the contact section below. Include device model, OS version, TapRush version (from the App Store / Play listing or in-app if shown), what you were doing, and a screenshot if it helps. The more specific the steps to reproduce, the faster we can investigate.
