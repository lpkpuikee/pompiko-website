TapRush: Beat the Clock — Support

Tips and answers based on how TapRush works today. For privacy disclosures, see the TapRush Privacy Policy. Questions: pompiko.support@gmail.com.

Getting Started

TapRush is a portrait reflex arcade game with a continuous journey from Level 1 to Level 1000. Clear each level by tapping valid targets as quickly as you can. Your clear time (with a small adjustment for lives lost) earns up to 3 stars — or 0 stars if you clear slowly.

  • From Home, check CURRENT LEVEL, then tap PLAY to continue, or open Level Select to revisit cleared levels.
  • Every attempt starts with three lives. Empty-space taps and bomb hits cost a life.
  • The run timer starts on your first valid hit (not when the level loads).
  • Progress (stars, unlocks, best times, settings) is saved on your device. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I play TapRush?

Tap the valid target in the play area. Some levels also show bomb decoys — tapping a bomb costs a life. Empty-space taps also cost a life. Finish all required targets before you run out of lives. On the results screen you can advance, retry, or share when your device supports sharing.

2. How do levels unlock?

Clearing a level unlocks the next one in sequence from 1 through 1000 as one continuous journey. Level Select lets you replay completed levels and see stars / best times when recorded.

3. How does the lives system work?

Every level starts with three lives. Misses (including empty-space taps) and bomb hits cost a life. When you run out of lives, the run pauses and you may be offered continue options when those features are enabled for your build. You can always Retry the level for free or leave the run.

4. How do bombs work?

On bomb levels, one or two bombs can appear at the same time as a valid target. Bombs may be stationary or move more slowly than the target. Tap only the valid target — never the bomb.

5. How do combos and stars work?

Successful hits build an optional combo streak with milestone celebrations. Combos do not change your star rating. Stars use adjusted time: active gameplay time plus about two seconds per life lost. Finishing above the one-star gate still clears the level but awards 0 stars.

6. How can I earn extra lives after running out?

On Out of Lives, when continues are enabled for your platform build, you may be offered:

  • A rewarded advertisement — watch to completion to receive continue lives for that attempt (availability depends on ads, consent, and fill).
  • Optional in-app life packs (consumable continues) processed by the App Store or Google Play — TapRush never asks for your card number inside the game.

Retry remains free. Paid or ad continues grant temporary extra lives within the current run; they are not a permanent unlock of all future lives. Ads and purchases may be unavailable depending on region, consent, store status, or network conditions.

7. What happens if a rewarded advertisement is unavailable?

If an ad cannot start, needs privacy consent, has no fill, or fails to finish, TapRush does not grant lives. You’ll see an in-game message such as checking your connection, reviewing Ad Privacy in Settings, trying again later, or skipping to a paid continue when purchases are available. You can always Retry or exit without paying.

8. What should I do if my purchase doesn't appear?

Purchases are confirmed by Apple or Google, then verified by TapRush before lives are granted. If payment is still processing, wait for the store confirmation. If nothing appears after a successful charge:

  • Keep the app open on the same device and wait a moment for recovery.
  • Check that you are signed into the same App Store / Play account used to buy.
  • Force-quit and reopen TapRush once on the same device.
  • If lives still do not appear, contact support with your device details, approximate purchase time, product (life pack size), and platform — do not send passwords, full card numbers, or raw purchase receipts containing secrets.
9. How do sharing and audio settings work?

From Results, you can share a text (and, when supported, image) summary of your clear. In Settings you can toggle sound effects and background music independently, adjust volumes when available, and control haptics / reduced effects. Music ducks during ads and while the app is in the background.

10. How do I report a bug?

Use the contact section below. Include device model, OS version, TapRush version (from the store listing or in-app if shown), what you were doing, and a screenshot if it helps. The more specific the steps to reproduce, the faster we can investigate.
