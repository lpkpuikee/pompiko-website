2. Scope

This draft applies to the TapRush mobile applications for Android and iOS. It covers:

  • Information processed on your device
  • Information processed by advertising and store platforms when those features are enabled
  • Information processed by our purchase-verification service when in-app purchases are enabled

Web browser play, if any, may have different limits (for example, ads and store purchases may be unavailable).

3. Information we collect or process

3.1 Information stored on your device

TapRush stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device (for example via the app’s local storage), including:

  • Progress (unlocked levels, stars, best times, achievements)
  • Settings (sound, haptics, reduced effects)
  • Anonymous identifiers used only on-device for local analytics
  • Local gameplay and purchase-related analytics events (not used as a payment record)
  • Temporary purchase-recovery records (pending grants / life credits) when purchases are used. Pending grants may briefly include purchase proofs needed to finish granting lives after an interruption; those proofs are cleared when fulfillment completes or the local records are removed.
  • Server-issued installation credentials used to secure purchase verification when purchases are enabled

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or phone number to play.

3.2 Advertising (when ads are enabled)

When rewarded advertisements are enabled, Google’s advertising and consent components (AdMob / User Messaging Platform) may process device and advertising-related information according to Google’s policies and your consent choices. TapRush requests non-personalized ads in its rewarded-ad integration. That setting reduces ad personalization; it does not mean Google collects no data.

Exact technical fields collected by Google’s SDKs are defined by Google, not by TapRush’s custom game code. We do not claim that “no personal data is collected” by advertising partners. [VERIFY: production AdMob App/unit IDs must replace Google sample IDs before paid inventory ships.]

3.3 In-app purchases (when purchases are enabled)

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your full payment card details.

When our purchase-verification service is enabled, we may process:

  • Purchase product identifiers
  • Transaction identifiers
  • Cryptographic purchase proofs from Apple or Google (sent over HTTPS to verify; stored in our ledger as a hash, not as payment card numbers)
  • Platform / app identity checks
  • An anonymous installation credential issued by our service
  • Network-edge signals such as IP address, used to prevent abuse (for example rate limiting)

Localized price strings may be recorded in on-device analytics only. [VERIFY: D1 retention policy for ledger and rate-limit records before publish.]

3.4 Diagnostics

TapRush does not currently integrate a third-party crash-reporting SaaS in the shipped codebase. Your device operating system may generate its own crash or diagnostic logs outside this App. [Re-check if a crash SDK is added later]

3.5 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect: government ID numbers, precise continuous location for marketing, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or social-media friend lists.

4. How we use information

  • Operate gameplay, saves, and settings on your device
  • Provide optional rewarded ads and optional paid continues
  • Verify and fulfill in-app purchases and prevent duplicate grants
  • Improve balance and quality using on-device analytics (not uploaded to our servers in the current app; this policy will be updated if that changes)
  • Comply with law and platform requirements
  • Respond to support requests you send to our support email

5. Advertising and consent

In regions where required (including via Google’s User Messaging Platform), you may see a consent message before ads are requested.

Where available, you can open Ad Privacy in the App’s Settings to review or change privacy options provided by the consent platform.

If you do not consent where consent is required, ad-based continues may be unavailable. You can still Retry or Exit without paying.

App Tracking Transparency (Apple): TapRush’s current design does not present an ATT prompt and does not seek the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. If personalized ads or ATT are enabled later, this policy and App Store disclosures will be updated.

6. In-app purchases

Optional paid continues grant temporary extra lives within a run. Retry remains free. Consumable purchases are generally not “restored” like non-consumable unlocks after reinstall; same-device recovery mechanisms may apply when verification succeeds.

Purchases use Apple’s and Google’s terms and privacy practices in addition to this policy.

7. Service providers

Provider Purpose
Google (AdMob / UMP) Rewarded ads and consent messaging
Apple App Store / Google Play Payment processing for in-app purchases
Cloudflare (Workers / D1) Purchase verification and fulfillment ledger when enabled

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Storage and security

  • Gameplay progress is stored primarily on your device.
  • Purchase-verification records (when enabled) are stored with our cloud provider with access controls and hashed purchase tokens where implemented.
  • No security measure is perfect; we work to protect information using reasonable technical measures appropriate to a small mobile game.

11. Children’s privacy and age

TapRush is a skill-based tap game intended for a general audience (owner confirmed). It is not specifically directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children for account registration (we do not offer accounts).

Our AdMob integration does not enable “Designed for Families” / child-directed product modes in app code. Exact Google request-configuration defaults may still apply under Google’s policies. Store age ratings and regional rules may further restrict availability. 12. Your rights and requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing, or to object to certain uses.

Because most gameplay data is on your device, you can delete it by uninstalling the App or clearing app storage.

For requests related to purchase-verification records or support correspondence, email pompiko.support@gmail.com and include enough detail for us to locate relevant records (for example platform and approximate purchase time). We may need to verify your request.

We do not sell your personal information as “data brokers” sell lists. Advertising partners may use device signals for ads under their policies and your choices — see Google’s and Apple’s disclosures.
