Pompiko presents

TapRush: Beat the Clock

Tap fast. Stay sharp. Beat the clock.
A reflex-based mobile arcade game that challenges your speed, accuracy and concentration.

Gameplay features

  • 1,000 levels Progress through an increasingly challenging journey from Level 1 to Level 1000.
  • 20–200 targets Complete more targets as you advance through the journey.
  • Reflex challenges Hit moving targets while avoiding bomb decoys that appear alongside valid marks.
  • Combo streaks Build consecutive successful hits for milestone celebrations. Combos do not change star ratings.
  • Three lives Every level starts with three lives. Empty-space taps and bomb hits cost a life.
  • Beat your best time Earn up to three stars from your adjusted clear time (active time plus a small penalty for lives lost). A clear can still earn zero stars if you finish slowly.
  • Share your results Challenge friends to beat your performance from the results screen when sharing is available on your device.

How to play

  1. Tap the valid target in the play area.
  2. Avoid bombs and empty-space taps — each miss costs one life.
  3. Complete the required targets before you lose all three lives. The timer starts on your first valid hit.
  4. Improve your time, earn stars, and advance to the next unlocked level.
